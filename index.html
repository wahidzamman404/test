PEHLE, A FEW ANSWERS

A little clarity.
A lot more sukoon.

Need to talk through a detail?
Let’s have a conversation

Can I order custom furniture for my home?

Yes—the collection is a starting point for discussing dining sets, baithak seating, beds, wardrobes, doors, cabinets, jali panels, and other woodwork. Share your measurements and ideas so a design can be discussed.

Do I have to know the exact measurements?

Approximate dimensions are enough for an initial inquiry. Final measurements, doorway access, and installation details need to be confirmed with the workshop before production.

Can I choose the wood and polish?

Tell us whether you prefer sheesham, deodar, ash, or another material, and whether you like a natural, darker, or painted finish. Suitability, stock, and a final sample should be agreed for each project.

How do I get a price in Pakistani rupees?

Share the piece, approximate size, preferred material, and your city. A quotation in PKR can then be discussed with the business. Pricing depends on the final specification; this demo does not offer checkout or fixed prices.

What about delivery and installation?

Include your city in the inquiry—Lahore, Islamabad, Karachi, or elsewhere in Pakistan. Coverage, delivery charges, installation, and timelines need to be confirmed in the quotation before placing an order.
